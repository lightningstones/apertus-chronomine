# Dataset Card: ChronoMine AI Subterranean Evaluation Benchmark

## Dataset Summary
This evaluation dataset benchmarks the **Apertus v1.5** foundation model family (`swiss-ai/Apertus-v1.5-70B` and `8B`) on Swiss underground civil engineering, norm compliance, and life-safety auditing for the **Hack Apertus 2026 (Track 2B: Own Project)**.

It evaluates two mission-critical tasks in deep Alpine tunnels and military defense caverns (*Felskavernen*):
1. **Task A: Multilingual SIA 118 Bautagebuch & Regierapport Structuring** (Frontline field logs in vernacular Portuguese, Italian, Spanish translated and structured into official Swiss German and French daily logs with force-account claims under SIA 118 Art. 44).
2. **Task B: SUVA & BauAV 2022 Safety Violation Auditing** (Detecting life-threatening excavation hazards, missing trench shoring > 1.50m under BauAV Art. 54, three-part scaffolding edge protection under BauAV Art. 15, and triggering mandatory Stop-Work Orders).

## Files in this Repository
- `evaluation_dataset.json`: The test cases with ground-truth labels and evaluation metrics.
- `model_response.json`: The actual responses generated by `swiss-ai/Apertus-v1.5-70B` via the sovereign Swisscom AI Platform.
- `metadata.json`: Dataset metadata, curation details, and licensing references.

## Languages
The dataset covers multilingual input (Portuguese, Italian, Spanish, German, French) reflecting the actual frontline demographic of subterranean Swiss civil engineering workforces.
